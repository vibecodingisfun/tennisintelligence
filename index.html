Tennis Intelligence

See the game in the data.

Tennis Intelligence uses data science to visualize how tennis is actually played. The database is the match record itself: results, serve lines, and shot-by-shot charts, drawn so a pattern is something you can point at.

  • 495,128tour matches, ATP and WTA
  • 16,582players in those matches
  • 55,777Grand Slam matches
  • 7,088matches charted shot by shot

ATP matches run from 1967, WTA matches from 1990, both through September 2026. 258,122 matches include a complete serve line. The charts use the shot-by-shot set: 91 men with at least 40 charted matches, and 46 women. Forehand speed is the smaller picture, 19 men with a published tracking average.
